# VisualShaderNodeFresnel

**Inherits:**  
- VisualShaderNode  
- Resource  
- RefCounted  
- Object  

**Description**  
- Returns falloff based on the dot product of surface normal and view direction of camera. Passes associated inputs to it.  

**Key Functionality**  
- Computes reflectivity based on surface normal and camera view direction.  
- Used in visual shader graphs for realistic lighting effects.

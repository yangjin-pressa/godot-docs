**Class Name**: VideoStreamTheora  
**Inherits**:  
- VideoStream  
  - Resource  
    - RefCounted  
      - Object  

**Description**:  
- Handles Ogg Theora video format with `.ogv` extension.  
- Theora codec is decoded on the CPU.  

**Note**:  
- While Ogg Theora videos may use `.ogg` extension, they must be renamed to `.ogv` to function in Godot.  

**Reference**:  
- [Theora website](https://www.theora.org/)
